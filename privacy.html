Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SANO Med Spa (“SANO,” a DBA of Lifetime Wellness, LLC) collects, uses, and protects the information you provide through our websites, landing pages, and forms, including our online assessment and appointment-booking tools.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide — such as your name, email address, phone number, and the responses you give in our assessment and booking forms — along with basic usage and device information collected automatically through standard analytics tools.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, schedule and confirm your appointment, provide and improve our services, and — where you have opted in — send you appointment-related and promotional communications by email and text message.

Cookies & Tracking

We and our analytics and advertising partners use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to operate our site, measure performance, and understand how visitors use our pages. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. We may use tools such as Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, and the Meta (Facebook) pixel; these tools may collect device and usage information subject to their own privacy policies.

SMS / Text Messaging

By providing your mobile number and checking our consent box, you agree to receive text messages from SANO Med Spa, including appointment scheduling, reminders, and promotional offers. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message frequency varies — typically 2–6 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time, or HELP for assistance.

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in consent with any third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Your mobile information is used solely to deliver the messages you have requested from SANO Med Spa.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers (for example, scheduling, communications, and analytics vendors) who process it on our behalf and are bound by confidentiality obligations. As stated above, your mobile opt-in information and SMS consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect the information you provide. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any email, and contact us to access, correct, or delete your personal information.
