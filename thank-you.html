Make it count

Come ready and you'll get more out of it.

  • Bring your medication list Anything you take regularly, including supplements. It matters for how we approach your plan.
  • Think about what you've already tried The programs, the timelines, what worked for a while and what didn't. It's the most useful thirty seconds of the whole conversation.
  • Write down your questions Side effects, cost, what happens after six months — all of it is fair game. People forget in the room. Bring the list.
  • Know your goal, not just a number "Off my blood pressure medication" or "keep up with my kids" is more useful to us than a target weight.
