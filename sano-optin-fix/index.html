That leak isn't the real problem — it's a warning light.

It's not because you're getting older, and it's not because you didn't do enough Kegels. It is not your fault.

Here's the real problem.

Deep inside your body is a hammock of muscles — your pelvic floor. It holds up your bladder and your core. After childbirth, menopause, and the years in between, those muscles weaken — and the leak is simply the first sign they've started to give way. For many women, those muscles don't strengthen again on their own.

And little by little, you shrink your life to hide it — the workout you skip, the trip you talk yourself out of, every outing planned around the nearest bathroom. Each year, it quietly takes a little more of your core, your confidence, and your freedom.

So why hasn't anything worked?

Those deep muscles are nearly impossible to activate on your own. Your Kegels squeeze the muscles you can feel — while the ones that actually matter barely move. It was never a willpower problem. It's anatomy.

But those muscles can be reached.

It's the Nupelva Pelvic Floor Reset. You sit down, fully clothed, and in one 28-minute session, Nupelva uses focused electromagnetic energy to stimulate the deep pelvic floor muscles — the ones that are hard to activate on your own. It's non-invasive: no surgery, no needles, no downtime. Individual results vary, and your provider will discuss what's realistic for you.

Questions women ask us

Does it hurt? What does it feel like?

Most women describe it as a gentle tingling or tapping sensation. You stay fully clothed and comfortable the entire time — many relax, read, or scroll their phone.

Do I have to undress?

No. You remain fully clothed for the entire session. It's private, comfortable, and there's no exam involved.

How many sessions will I need?

Your $99 first session is designed for you to experience the treatment. Your provider will map out a personalized plan at your visit based on your goals and history. The number of sessions varies from person to person.

Is this only for bladder leaks?

No. The pelvic floor muscles play a role in bladder control, core stability and intimacy, so women come to us for a range of reasons. Your provider will tailor the plan to your goals at your visit.

What makes Nupelva different?

Nupelva uses focused electromagnetic energy to stimulate the pelvic floor muscles while you sit fully clothed. Your provider will explain how it works and whether it's appropriate for you at your visit.

Is it safe? Who oversees it?

Treatments at Sano are overseen by our Medical Director, Dr. Andrew Mahtani, MD, and delivered by our trained clinical team. The quiz screens for the few situations where we'd want to talk with you first.

What's the $99 first session?

It's a new-client intro so you can experience the treatment for yourself before committing to a plan. If you continue, your $99 is credited toward your package.
